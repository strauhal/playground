DESIGN INTENT

An open centre. A shared view.

One hundred circular floors trace four deeply pinched hourglass forms inspired by your red-marked sketch. Four broad concrete stair ribbons now connect the platforms, with transparent guards, intermediate landings and a bridge to every floor. The straight vertical ribs are removed. Each bay spans 25 stories, sweeping from a broad 200-metre ring into a roughly 50-metre waist, then opening outward again.

Inward-facing terraces and a continuous central opening follow the changing silhouette. The atrium narrows to about 27.5 metres at the waists and widens to 110 metres at the main bands. The 6-metre floor spacing gives a total height of 600 metres.

“Two blocks” is interpreted as 200 metres across in both horizontal directions, using an imagined 100-metre block. Use Open front and Terrace detail to explore the interior. Exterior stairs are an artistic circulation concept, not an engineered or code-reviewed stair system.
