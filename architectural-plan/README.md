## Outer connecting frame revision
The outer edge strokes from the original sculpture are interpreted as eight continuous, gently bowed concrete ribs, paired sweeping braces between major platforms, circular connecting bands at 0/150/300/450/600 m and radial ties every five floors. This is a regularized artistic interpretation, not an exact tracing or a structural analysis. ## Exterior stairway revision
Straight outer ribs, narrow curved facade ribs, and stacked circulation markers have been removed from the proposed model. Four continuous stair ribbons (same handedness, quarter-turn apart) replace the external frame. Each ribbon is 4 m wide, with 0.5 m visual concrete thickness and 1.2 m transparent guards. Each 6 m floor interval has two flights of 18 rises, intermediate and floor landings, with a 4 m-wide radial bridge to every floor. Imagined rise is 1/6 m, and angular advance is 0.16 radians per floor at radius 96 m. The stair centreline remains continuous between intervals and reaches the roof. The rings and hourglass terraces remain. These are artistic dimensions and do not establish engineering or code compliance.
